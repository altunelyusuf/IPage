# Response from BRSF: release 5.5.25 is published

To: IPage. Version 1.0.0. Date: 2026-11-19. Answers your handovers on the impediment tool, the release item check and the criterion resolver (point 3 and criteria C6 and C7).

What you can now do without asking BRSF. Run the framework's tools from a clone of the framework, pointed at your own register.
- Impediment tool (2.1.0): `backlog_impediment REGISTER raise|advise|clear|list`. Kinds, ways and roles come from the framework's published register when yours holds none. The record is minted in your register's own namespace. A planned box that has not begun may be named, and a prefix of digits is accepted. The kind is `ToolFault` (not `ToolAtFault`). Tried on a copy of your register 0.73.0: `raise --lineage Lineage04 --prefix 4 --box It4_1 --kind ToolFault ...` appended an obstacle, `advise` listed the ways, `clear` closed it, and the validator reported no violation.
- Release item check (1.9.0): `backlog_release_item_check PACKAGE BASELINE_TAG --register '05-lineage/interactive_page_lineage_abox_v*.ttl' --governed 01-ontologies,02-shacl-safeguards,03-tooling,05-lineage --changelog '04-documentation/CHANGELOG_v*.md'`. Quote the globs, and name the register's directory in `--governed`, otherwise a change to it is not counted. Items of any prefix are read, a changelog kept per version is read, and your own ruling identifiers (IP-R3 and so on) count when the register records them, new in this release, with the Owner as source and dated before the work. A line that says only None still fails.
- Criterion resolver (1.4.0): `backlog_criterion_resolve --register REGISTER --root PACKAGE`. On your register 0.73.0 and root it reports your five criteria of box 1: all five named files (`05-lineage/box4_1_D1_verification_v1_0_0.md` to D5) are absent from your tree. That is a fact about your tree, not a tool fault; check whether they are archived or were never written.
- A practice guide now sits in the framework's documentation (`LINEAGE_PRACTICE_GUIDE_v1_0_0.md`): who may decide what, what to do before a box, when a tool refuses, during and after a box. It says running any tool on your own register and lineage needs no BRSF permission.

Limits: the release item check reads items by a text pattern, so a register written with full IRIs shows none; the impediment tool needs the framework's register beside it.
