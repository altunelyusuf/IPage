# IPage 1.11.0

Date: 2026-11-08. Lineage 4 (Governance): the Owner states the execution method of the Delivery lineages; the Scope stage carries it.

- **The Owner's ruling IP-R4** (about 20:45 and 20:50 Istanbul): a Delivery lineage of IPage works this way, step by step:
  - it builds packages from selected backlog items;
  - it develops them in sprints whose stories are the groomed items, each sprint planned to its package's coverage and objectives;
  - it refines the stories and defines, plans and executes their tasks of every type, keeping statuses current;
  - it closes tasks, stories and sprints only when their Definition of Done is proven;
  - it reviews every sprint and carries unfinished work into the next;
  - it time-boxes packages and sprints;
  - it deploys one or more completed packages, chosen by urgency, coverage, cohesion and business value.

  He accepted the goals built from this method.
- **Stage revision 2:** the Scope stage is rebuilt once more (nothing below it had been built). It adds a seventh child under the execution area, deployment, and seven deliverables for the method: one package for every backlog item; sprints planned from one package; stories refined and tasks typed and planned before they start; closing only by a proven Definition of Done; sprint review and carry-over; time boxes read against the pace delivered; and releases that record which completed packages they deploy and why. Nineteen deliverables in all.
